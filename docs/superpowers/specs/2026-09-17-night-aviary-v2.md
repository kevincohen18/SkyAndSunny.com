# Night Aviary V2 — Moonlit Clearing

**Status:** Approved iteration after V1 visual rejection
**Date:** 2026-09-17

## Why V1 was rejected

V1 read as a conventional brochure on a dark teal background: a headline/image split, repeated portrait rows, and a thumbnail collection. The bird theme lived in the content but did not determine the space. It lacked enclosure, depth, moonlight, foliage, and a convincing perch; mobile delayed the birds behind marketing copy.

## Locked direction

The complete page becomes one edge-to-edge moonlit aviary diorama. A three-plane branching canopy frames a clearing where the authentic Sunny and Sky portraits live. Scrolling follows the same irregular branch through three large openings containing their real TikTok moments.

The design must be unmistakably bird-specific in a still screenshot. Animation enhances depth but cannot carry the concept.

## First viewport

- Edge-to-edge navy-black habitat; no centered website rectangle or hard section band.
- Back plane: matte moon, twelve restrained stars, and faint curved aviary ribs.
- Middle plane: branching silhouettes and large two-line `Sky & Sunny` title.
- Front plane: substantial foliage entering from the viewport edges, authentic Sunny and Sky portrait cutouts, and one believable shared branch.
- Both bird faces appear within the first 390×844 mobile viewport.
- The birds remain still. Foreground scenery may move by at most 6px on fine pointers.
- `Two tiny birds. One very big screen presence.` becomes supporting copy rather than the dominant visual.

## Portrait treatment

- Use the existing authentic portrait derivatives.
- Apply separate subject-following SVG/CSS masks; never generic circles, arches, rounded rectangles, or blobs.
- Keep Sunny’s feet and tail visible.
- Keep Sky’s real diagonal perch and align the illustrated branch continuation to it.
- Faces receive comparable visual prominence; Sky cannot become a small secondary sticker.
- The hero itself introduces the birds. Remove the duplicated Meet section entirely.

## Scrolling habitat

- One continuous branch descends through three encounters: watermelon, strawberries, then the tall obsessions/corn scene.
- Landscape moments occupy roughly 55–65% of desktop width on alternating sides.
- The portrait moment remains tall and wide enough to show both Sunny and the screen.
- Branches and foreground foliage overlap selected photograph corners to make openings in the habitat.
- Preserve each domestic image as a complete scene; do not pretend it was photographed outdoors.
- Captions and visible TikTok links sit in adjacent clearings. No cards, white frames, badges, fake play controls, or fabricated counts.

## Visual system

- Deep night `#071016` canvas.
- Distant canopy `#142B2B`.
- Moon/primary text `#EEEAD7`.
- Quiet text `#BAC9C3`.
- Sunny accent `#E7C769`.
- Sky/focus accent `#81D3D8`.
- Use the authentic photos for all additional colour.
- A firm-edged physical moon replaces glows and gradients.
- Irregular solid branches may receive one restrained moonlit edge.
- Use Fraunces for the main title and Source Sans 3 for interface/body copy, with resilient fallbacks. Two families maximum.

## Motion

- One entrance: closest canopy settles outward by 10–14px over 700–900ms.
- Fine pointer only: foreground branches and moon oppose each other by no more than 6px.
- Focusing or hovering a moment may withdraw one adjacent twig by 3–4px; the link remains visible before interaction.
- No moving birds, flight trails, cursor feathers, audio, perpetual star particles, scroll-reveal parade, or wobbling photographs.
- Reduced motion renders the final composition immediately with no transforms or smooth scrolling.

## Mobile translation

- Preserve the diorama rather than collapsing into headline/buttons/photos.
- Compact title near top; both bird faces in the first viewport; branch occupies its lower area; primary action follows immediately.
- Simplify canopy cropping while retaining all three depth planes.
- Moments become full-width openings linked by a branch near one edge.
- Avoid desktop-derived empty side space and text collisions at 320px or 200% zoom.

## Remove from V1

- Dominant promotional heading treatment and hero kicker.
- Gull-like marks, floating name badges, arbitrary portrait radii, straight brown bars.
- Separate Meet section, repeated portraits, and `Two portraits, two unmistakable palettes.`
- Thumbnail/grid composition, hard teal bands, opaque bordered header strip, and repeated card-like image treatments.

## Keep from V1

- Five verified local media derivatives.
- Exact three TikTok destinations and honest captions.
- Semantic landmarks, accessible menu, skip link, JS-off content, reduced-motion foundation, and local static architecture.

## Acceptance

- At 1440×900 and 390×844, the first viewport reads as an inhabitable moonlit aviary with Sky and Sunny as residents.
- Both bird faces are visible in the first mobile viewport.
- The page reads as one continuous environment rather than stacked sections.
- All three real moments remain legible and keep their original bird–screen relationship.
- With animation disabled and text mentally removed, the habitat idea remains obvious.
- Existing structural, mapping, keyboard, JS-off, reduced-motion, 200% reflow, and failure checks still pass.
